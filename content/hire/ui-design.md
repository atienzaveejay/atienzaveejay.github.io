## ROO, a Shopify store for a US kitchen brand

ROO sells a silicone mat that sits under a cutting board and catches scraps. I redesigned the homepage and product pages and built them as Shopify sections. Live at [thisisroo.com](https://thisisroo.com).

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/roo-hero.webp" class="shot" data-group="roo"><img src="/assets/ui/roo-hero.webp" alt="ROO homepage hero"></a><figcaption>The photo shows the product doing its job, so the headline can stay short. Rating, price and free shipping all sit above the fold.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="phones two"><figure><a href="/assets/ui/roo-home-m.webp" class="shot" data-group="roo"><img src="/assets/ui/roo-home-m.webp" alt="ROO homepage on a phone"></a></figure><figure><a href="/assets/ui/roo-pdp-m.webp" class="shot" data-group="roo"><img src="/assets/ui/roo-pdp-m.webp" alt="ROO product page on a phone"></a><figcaption>On phones, price and Add to Cart stay pinned to the bottom of the screen.</figcaption></figure></div>

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/roo-setup.webp" class="shot" data-group="roo"><img src="/assets/ui/roo-setup.webp" alt="ROO pick your setup cards"></a><figcaption>Two clear choices instead of a product grid. The bundle gets the filled button and the outline, and the mat-only card says who it's for.</figcaption></figure>

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/roo-wall.webp" class="shot" data-group="roo"><img src="/assets/ui/roo-wall.webp" alt="ROO customer video wall"></a><figcaption>Real customer and creator videos replaced the two studio demo clips. Each one is labelled with the product it shows.</figcaption></figure>

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/roo-compare.webp" class="shot" data-group="roo"><img src="/assets/ui/roo-compare.webp" alt="ROO comparison table"></a><figcaption>A comparison against the two other ways to catch scraps, with ROO's column tinted so the eye lands there first.</figcaption></figure>

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/roo-built.webp" class="shot" data-group="roo"><img src="/assets/ui/roo-built.webp" alt="ROO built to last section"></a><figcaption>A dark band breaks up a long product page and holds the warranty and spec sheet.</figcaption></figure>

**Behind it:** the old site ran on two design systems with two typefaces and 33 font sizes. I replaced them with one set of tokens for spacing, type and colour, and checked every colour pair for contrast. That fixed star ratings and a main button that were both at 1.45:1, which is close to invisible.

## Winning Coaching, a workshop sales page

Coach Edwin Soriano coaches Filipino business owners. I designed and built the registration page for his paid two-hour workshop on Zoom, then carried the same look into the sales video, emails and his slide template.

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/wc-hero.webp" class="shot" data-group="wc"><img src="/assets/ui/wc-hero.webp" alt="Winning Coaching workshop page hero"></a><figcaption>The headline is the owner's problem in their own words, and the gold italic carries the turn. Price, date and countdown all sit next to the one button.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="phones two"><figure><a href="/assets/ui/wc-m.webp" class="shot" data-group="wc"><img src="/assets/ui/wc-m.webp" alt="Winning Coaching page on a phone"></a><figcaption>On phones the photo moves up and the headline still lands in the first screen.</figcaption></figure></div>

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/wc-offer.webp" class="shot" data-group="wc"><img src="/assets/ui/wc-offer.webp" alt="What the workshop includes"></a><figcaption>Three cards for what the price buys. The middle one is the reason to come, so it gets the gold outline and a label.</figcaption></figure>

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/wc-fix.webp" class="shot" data-group="wc"><img src="/assets/ui/wc-fix.webp" alt="What you leave knowing"></a><figcaption>The four things an owner leaves knowing, as a numbered grid with one line icon each.</figcaption></figure>

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/wc-dates.webp" class="shot" data-group="wc"><img src="/assets/ui/wc-dates.webp" alt="Workshop key dates"></a><figcaption>Dates on a timeline, so buyers see the refund cut-off and how long the replay stays up without reading a paragraph.</figcaption></figure>

**Behind it:** a premium look for a premium price: near-black ink, one gold, Geist for text and Newsreader italic for the words that matter. The page is a single custom code block inside GoHighLevel, so it loads like a hand-built site while the form and payments stay in the client's system.

## Kareera, an AI mock interview app

My own product: an AI interviewer for Filipino job seekers. I made the brand, the interface and the app. Live at [kareera.co](https://kareera.co).

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/kareera-hero.webp" class="shot" data-group="kareera"><img src="/assets/ui/kareera-hero.webp" alt="Kareera homepage hero"></a><figcaption>Purple for actions, mint for the one thing to press. The hero shows the interview itself, so people see the product before they read about it.</figcaption></figure>

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/kareera-modes.webp" class="shot" data-group="kareera"><img src="/assets/ui/kareera-modes.webp" alt="Kareera interview modes"></a><figcaption>Four ways to practise on one grid. Video call, the newest and closest to a real interview, gets the biggest card.</figcaption></figure>

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/kareera-people.webp" class="shot" data-group="kareera"><img src="/assets/ui/kareera-people.webp" alt="Kareera interviewer personas"></a><figcaption>Three interviewers, each introduced by a line they'd actually say. Nervous applicants can start with the gentle one.</figcaption></figure>

<figure class="ui-fig"><a href="/assets/ui/kareera-score.webp" class="shot" data-group="kareera"><img src="/assets/ui/kareera-score.webp" alt="Kareera feedback screen"></a><figcaption>Feedback reads as worked and missing, then a stronger version of the person's own answer, then one button to try again.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="phones two"><figure><a href="/assets/ui/kareera-m.webp" class="shot" data-group="kareera"><img src="/assets/ui/kareera-m.webp" alt="Kareera homepage on a phone"></a></figure></div>

**Behind it:** the brand is built to calm people down before an interview, so the palette is quiet and the racing theme in the name only shows up in small touches, like the slanted wordmark. The logo is a solid badge because the plain line version blurred at favicon size.

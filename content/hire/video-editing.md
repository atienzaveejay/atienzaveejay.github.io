## How I work

- You send the raw footage and the script or notes. I send a first cut with captions, music and graphics.
- Notes come back as timestamps. I fix each one and tell you what changed.
- Shorts come from the same masters as the long video, so the look matches across the channel.
- Happy to do a paid test edit on your own footage first.

## What I handle

- Talking-head cuts from raw teleprompter or Zoom takes, with retakes and stutters removed
- Word-timed captions and on-screen text
- Music beds, sound effects and audio levels
- Diagrams and simple motion graphics timed to what the speaker says
- Shorts, Reels and TikToks cut from long-form videos
- Product Reels and Meta video ads from phone footage
- YouTube thumbnails and titles

Tools: DaVinci Resolve and CapCut.

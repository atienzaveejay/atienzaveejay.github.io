## What I make

- Carousels and social media posts for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn
- Pinterest pins
- Promo, giveaway, event and announcement graphics
- YouTube thumbnails
- Reusable templates so a brand's posts stay consistent

Tools: Canva, plus CapCut when a post needs motion.

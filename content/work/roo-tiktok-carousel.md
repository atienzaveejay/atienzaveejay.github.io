## Context

ROO's TikTok was getting about 1,600 views a week across all posts. Video production wasn't realistic on the budget, so I looked for a format that could be built from stock and candid photos and still feel native to the feed.

## The format

I reverse-engineered a genre that was already working on TikTok: the "safety tips from someone who cares about you" photo carousel. Bold white text over unpolished photos, a personal hook on slide one, practical tips in the middle, and the product mentioned once, near the end, as part of the story rather than an ad.

The version that broke out was told through a father who ran a restaurant kitchen for 20 years:

I turned it into a six-slide template (face-visible hook, four anonymous tips, the product on slide five, a closing line) and built about 25 variants on it.

## The week it took off

@[bars](Aug 2 to 8=1,605; Aug 9 to 15=34,527; Aug 16 to 22=16,222 | Weekly TikTok views)

Profile views went from 20 to 373 in the same week, and likes from 53 to 252.

## The narrator test

A spike can be luck, so I tested what was actually doing the work. In the same week we posted two carousels in the identical format but narrated by outside experts (a butcher on raw meat rules, a fire marshal on grease fires), then a rebuilt family version.

@[bars](Butcher (expert)=730; Fire marshal (expert)=448; Dad's knives (family), day 1=3,923; Dad's knives (family), day 2=5,425 | Views per day, same week, same format)

The family narrator beat the credentialed experts by about eight times, and the family post also got the most shares of any day in the 60-day file. Borrowed authority works through intimacy, not credentials. That became a rule for every build after it.

## What didn't work

> 63,742 views produced one website click in 60 days.

Reporting views alone would have been flattering and useless, so I said so in the founder report and changed what we track. The weekly report now leads with profile views and link clicks, and the next tests target the conversion step (the profile, the bio and the link) rather than reach. A small paid test on TikTok drove no orders either.

## Takeaway

- Copy a proven genre, then test which element carries it. Here it was the narrator, not the tips.
- A credential is less persuasive in the feed than a person the viewer can picture.
- Views are a top-of-funnel number. If they don't move profile visits and clicks, say so early and change the test.

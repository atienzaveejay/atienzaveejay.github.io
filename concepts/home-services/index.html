How to spot a storm chaser

After big hail, out-of-state crews knock on every door. Be careful if someone:

  • Wants a signature before going on the roof
  • Offers to "take care of" your deductible
  • Has no local address you can drive to
  • Wants a big deposit before materials arrive
  • Can't show a certificate of insurance today

Questions after a storm

Something else? Call or text (972) 555-0142.

Do I need to be home?

No. We only need the yard and a safe spot for the ladder. If you want us to check the attic for leaks, someone needs to be there.

Which storm damaged my roof?

We check hail reports for your street and put the likely date in your report. Your insurer will ask for it.

Will a claim raise my premium?

That depends on your policy, so ask your agent. We can tell you whether the damage is real and big enough to be worth a claim.

What if the claim is denied?

We'll compare the adjuster's report with our photos. You can ask for a second inspection, and we'll be on the roof for that one too.

Is there a deadline to file?

Many Texas policies allow one to two years from the storm, but some are shorter. Check your policy, and don't wait on a leak.

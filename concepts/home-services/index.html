How an insurance roof claim works with us

Most hail jobs in DFW are paid by insurance. Here is what happens from the first call to the final check, and who does what.

  1. Day 1 to 3

    Free inspection

    We go up, test each slope and send you the photo report. If it isn't worth a claim, we tell you and you're done.

  2. Same week

    You call your insurance company

    You open the claim, not us. Have the storm date and our report handy. We'll help you find the date if you're not sure.

  3. 1 to 3 weeks

    We meet your adjuster on the roof

    Jess Teague walks the roof with the adjuster and points out every hit we found. Most adjusters see the same thing when they're shown it.

  4. After approval

    We go over the estimate with you

    We read the insurance estimate line by line, flag anything missed (drip edge, vents, code items) and send our documentation to the carrier.

  5. One day, most homes

    Install day

    Tear-off, new underlayment and shingles, cleanup with a magnet sweep for nails. You pay your deductible and the first insurance check.

  6. 2 to 4 weeks after

    Final check and warranty

    We send the final invoice so your carrier releases the held-back money, and register the manufacturer warranty in your name.

How to spot a storm chaser

After big hail, crews from out of state knock on every door. Be careful if someone:

  • Asks you to sign before they've been on the roof
  • Offers to "take care of" your deductible
  • Has no local address you can drive to
  • Wants a big deposit before materials arrive
  • Can't show a certificate of insurance today

Questions we get after a storm

Something else? Text or call (972) 555-0142.

Do I need to be home for the inspection?

No. We only need access to the yard and a safe spot for the ladder. If you'd like us to look in the attic for leaks, someone needs to be there.

How do I know which storm damaged my roof?

We check hail reports for your street and put the most likely date in your report. Your insurance company will ask for it when you open the claim.

Will filing a claim raise my premium?

That depends on your policy and carrier, so ask your agent. What we can tell you is whether the damage is real and big enough to be worth a claim. If it's not, we'll say so.

What if the adjuster denies my claim?

We'll go over their report with you and compare it with our photos. You can ask your carrier for a second inspection, and we'll be on the roof for that one too.

How long does a replacement take?

One day for most homes. Big or steep roofs can take two. We cover the yard and beds with tarps and run a magnet over the lawn and driveway when we're done.

Is there a deadline to file?

Many Texas policies give you one to two years from the storm date, but some are shorter. Check your policy, and don't wait on a leak.

Can I use Azelaic 10 with retinal or retinol?

Yes. Azelaic 10 in the morning, retinal at night. If skin gets tight, drop the retinal to every other night for a week.

How long before I see a difference?

Redness often settles in two to four weeks. Dark marks take closer to eight. Take a photo in the same light on day one.

Is it safe for sensitive skin or rosacea?

Azelaic acid is often recommended for rosacea, and everything here is fragrance free. Patch test on your jaw for three nights first, and ask your dermatologist if you see one.

Are you cruelty free?

Yes, and we do not sell where animal testing is required. Every formula is vegan except the Barrier Cream, which has beeswax.

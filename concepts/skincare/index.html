Can I use Azelaic 10 with my retinal or retinol?

Yes. Use Azelaic 10 in the morning and the retinal at night. If your skin gets tight, drop the retinal to every other night for a week.

How long before I see a difference?

Redness usually settles in two to four weeks. Dark marks take longer, often eight weeks of daily use. Take a photo in the same light on day one so you can compare.

Is it safe for sensitive skin or rosacea?

Azelaic acid is often recommended for rosacea, and everything here is fragrance free. Patch test on your jaw for three nights first. If you are under a dermatologist's care, check with them.

Are you cruelty free?

Yes. We do not test on animals and we do not sell in markets that require it. Every formula is vegan except the Barrier Cream, which has beeswax.

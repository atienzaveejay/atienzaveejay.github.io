Your first class

  1. Ten minutes with a coachCome fifteen minutes early. We ask about old injuries and your work setup, then watch you squat and bend with no weight.
  2. Learn one liftSquat, deadlift or press with an empty bar or a kettlebell. Never a weight you have to fight. Your coach watches every set and writes your numbers down.
  3. Shower and goTowels, four private showers and hair dryers. That night your coach messages you a plan for your next three classes.

Never lifted before? Most members hadn't. After the free class you join a four-week Barbell Basics block, then move up to Strength when your coach says you're ready.

    What your first class is like

    Most people who walk in have never touched a barbell. Here is the hour, minute by minute, for a 6:15 PM class.

    1. 6:00 PM

      Arrive and change

      Come 15 minutes early. Front desk gives you a towel and a locker key. Shoes off at the door, we train in flat shoes or socks.

    2. 6:05 PM

      Ten minutes with your coach

      One-on-one before the group starts. We ask about old injuries and your work setup, then check how you squat and hinge with no weight.

    3. 6:15 PM

      Warm-up with the group

      Ten minutes of mobility for stiff hips and shoulders, the usual damage from sitting all day.

    4. 6:25 PM

      Main lift

      Squat, deadlift or press. You start with an empty bar or a kettlebell, never a weight you have to grind. The coach watches every set and writes your numbers on the board.

    5. 6:55 PM

      Short finisher

      Eight to ten minutes on the bike, sled or carries. Hard, but you pick the pace.

    6. 7:15 PM

      Shower and go

      Four private showers with shampoo and body wash. Your coach messages you a plan for your next three classes the same night.

    What to bring

    • Clothes you can squat in
    • Flat shoes, or train in socks
    • A water bottle (we have a refill station)

    Questions

    Message us on (02) 8000 0000 if yours isn't here.

    I've never lifted. Can I still join?

    Yes, most members hadn't. Start with the free class, then a 4-week Barbell Basics block. You can join Strength after that.

    Is there parking?

    The building's pay parking is ₱60 for the first three hours. Members get a ₱40 validation at the front desk. Most people walk from their office.

    Can I come in my lunch break and still look decent after?

    That's what Lunch Express is for. It ends at 1:00 sharp. We have four showers, hair dryers, and a vanity counter with an iron for shirts.

    Do you do one-on-one sessions?

    Yes. Private sessions are ₱2,200 each or ₱19,000 for ten, and good for injury rehab with Coach Bea.

    How do I pay?

    GCash, Maya, credit card or bank transfer. Monthly plans bill on the same date each month.

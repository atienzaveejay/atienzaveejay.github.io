Questions

Common questions

Something else? Email hello@hourfold.com. A person who works on the product answers, usually within a few hours on weekdays.

What happens when my free trial ends?

Nothing is charged until you add a card. If you don't, your account goes read-only and your data stays there for 90 days so you can export it.

Do you take a cut of client payments?

No. Card and ACH payments run through your own Stripe account at Stripe's normal fees (2.9% + 30¢ for US cards, 0.8% for ACH capped at $5). Hourfold adds nothing on top.

What if we grow past 20 people?

Agency covers up to 20 people. Past that, each extra person is $4 a month. Clients who only view and pay invoices never count.

Can contractors track time without seeing our rates?

Yes. Give them the Member role and they see their own hours and projects, but no rates, budgets or invoices.

Does it handle tax and other currencies?

Invoices support any currency, up to two tax lines, and tax IDs on the invoice. Reports convert to your home currency at the rate on the invoice date.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from Settings, no call needed. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month. Yearly plans are refunded for the unused months.

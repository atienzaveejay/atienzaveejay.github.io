The first visit: a one-hour assessment

It costs ₱500 and it is not an entrance test. It tells us which group fits your child and what to work on first.

  1. First 40 min

    Your child works with a tutor

    A short reading passage and a math sheet set one grade below and at their grade. No timer for Grades 1 to 3.

  2. Last 20 min

    The tutor talks with you

    What went well, where your child got stuck, and which group and days we suggest.

  3. Within 2 days

    A written summary on Viber or email

    Keep it even if you decide not to enroll.

Please bring

  • Your child's latest report card (Form 138)
  • One recent quiz or long test, even a low score

Questions parents ask

Can my child join in the middle of the school year?

Yes. Most children start after the first or second quarter report card. We place them after the assessment and bill from their first session, not from the start of the month.

Do you help with homework or teach ahead?

Both. Each session starts with whatever is due that week. The rest of the time goes to the skills from the assessment, and in exam weeks to review.

What if classes are suspended because of rain or a typhoon?

If QC suspends classes, that day's session moves to Google Meet at the usual time, or to Saturday if your child prefers coming in. You are never billed for a lost session.

Do you have online tutoring?

Only for suspension days and for students we already know. We find that young children do better at a table with a tutor beside them.

Will I know how my child is doing?

You get a short written update at the end of each quarter, plus a message from the tutor whenever something comes up. You can also sit in on a session.

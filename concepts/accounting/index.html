Questions people ask on the first call

Do I have to use Xero?

For the monthly plans, yes. It lets us see your bank feeds daily and lets you see the same numbers we do. If you're on MYOB or QuickBooks we move your file across as a one-off job.

Is GST included in your prices?

Yes. Every price on this page includes GST. If you're registered for GST you can claim it back on your BAS, and our fee is a tax deduction for the business.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Plans run month to month with 30 days' notice. When you leave, we give your new accountant everything they need.

I'm behind on BAS and tax returns. Can you still help?

That's most of our new clients. We work out what's missing, give you one fixed price for the catch-up and talk to the ATO about penalties and payment plans for you.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. We're upstairs at 401 Sydney Road, a short walk from Jewell station. Most clients meet us once, then keep in touch by phone and email.

What if my business grows past my plan?

We check your transaction count each quarter. If you move up a plan we tell you first and the new price starts the following month.

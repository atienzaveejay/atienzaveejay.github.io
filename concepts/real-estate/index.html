How we sell your house

The same agent looks after you from the first visit to settlement day. Most of our houses sell within four weeks of going online.

  1. Appraisal visit

    We walk through the house with you and point out what buyers here will ask about, like stumps, the roof and the pool fence.

  2. Written estimate

    Within two business days you get a price range and the sales it's based on. You keep it whether you list with us or not.

  3. Auction or private sale

    We tell you which one suits your house and why. Character homes on good blocks usually go to auction.

  4. Photos, floor plan and listing

    Online on realestate.com.au and Domain within a week, with a board out the front.

  5. A report after every open home

    Who came through, what they said about price and who's asked for a contract.

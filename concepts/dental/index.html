In network with Aetna, Cigna, Delta Dental, MetLife, UnitedHealthcare and nine more. We call your insurer before you come and text you what's covered. No insurance? Our in-house plan is $29 a month and covers two cleanings a year.

Nervous? Tell us.

You don't need to explain why. Mention it when you book and we'll give you extra time at no charge. Raise your hand and we stop, every time. Headphones and laughing gas are there if you want them.

Plenty of our patients hadn't been to a dentist in years. Nobody here will lecture you about it. If you'd like, come in first just to meet us and sit in the chair, with no treatment at all.

The whole family on one Saturday morning

We see kids from their first tooth, and parents can stay in the room. Book everyone back to back and make one drive instead of four.

Grandparents are welcome too. We look after dentures and partials, and Dr. Reyes and our front desk both speak Spanish.

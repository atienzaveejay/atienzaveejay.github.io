Your first visit

What happens at your first visit

Plan on an hour. Here's how that hour usually goes.

  1. 0:00

    Check in

    Your forms are already done on your phone. We confirm your insurance and offer you water and a blanket.

  2. 0:05

    Talk with your dentist

    Sitting up, not lying back. Tell us what's bothering you and anything that's gone badly at a dentist before.

  3. 0:15

    X-rays and photos

    Digital X-rays take about two minutes. We show you the pictures on the screen beside you.

  4. 0:25

    Cleaning

    Your hygienist goes at your pace. If your gums need a deeper cleaning, we'll tell you and book it for another day.

  5. 0:50

    Your plan and the price

    You leave with a written plan showing what insurance pays and what you pay. No work starts that day unless you ask for it.

Nervous patients

If the dentist makes you anxious

About one in three of our patients tells us they're nervous. You don't have to explain why. Tick the box when you book, or ask for any of these on the day.

  • A stop signal

    Raise your left hand and we stop right away. We agree on it before we start.

  • Headphones and a blanket

    Noise-cancelling headphones in every room. Bring your own playlist or use ours.

  • A longer appointment

    We book you 30 extra minutes at no charge so nobody is watching the clock.

  • Laughing gas

    Nitrous oxide wears off in minutes, so you can drive yourself home. $45 a visit.

  • Numbing you can't feel

    Numbing gel goes on first, then a slow, warmed injection. Most people say they missed it.

  • A visit with no treatment

    Come meet us and sit in the chair with nothing happening. It's free and takes 15 minutes.

Questions

Common questions

Something else? Text us at (512) 555-0142. We answer during office hours.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. Most new patients are seen within a week, and Saturday spots usually open up about two weeks out.

What does the $89 new patient visit include?

An exam with the dentist, a full set of digital X-rays and a regular cleaning. It's for patients without insurance. If you need a deep cleaning, that's priced separately and booked for another day.

How do I know what my insurance will pay?

Choose your plan when you book. We call your insurer before your visit and text you what's covered. At the visit you get a written estimate before any treatment.

I have a toothache right now. Can you see me today?

We hold emergency slots every weekday morning. Call before 10 am and we'll fit you in that day. The emergency exam is $75 and most insurance covers it.

What if I need to cancel?

Text or call at least 24 hours ahead. There's no fee the first time. Things happen.

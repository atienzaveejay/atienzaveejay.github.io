Groups and kamayan sets

Birthdays and barkada dinners get the long table by the grill. We lay the food on banana leaves and you eat with your hands (spoons if you ask).

Kamayan set for 4 to 6 ₱5,800

Liempo, inasal, inihaw na pusit, one whole fish, ensaladang talong, garlic rice and a pitcher of iced tea.

Kamayan set for 8 to 10 ₱9,600

Everything above in bigger portions, plus kare-kare and leche flan for the table.

Upstairs room, up to 16 ₱30,000 minimum

Private, with its own aircon and a speaker for your playlist. The minimum is spent on food and drinks.

  • Book kamayan sets at least two days ahead.
  • Groups of 8 or more pay a ₱2,000 deposit by GCash or bank transfer. It comes off your bill.
  • Bringing a cake is fine. No cake fee.

The kitchen

Chef Migs Santiago grew up on his lola's ihawan in Bacolod, then cooked for nine years in hotel kitchens in Makati and Singapore.

Baga is the food he missed while he was away. Our pork comes from a farm in Batangas, the fish from the Navotas port, and most of the vinegar from Ilocos and Iloilo. Everything that can go on the grill does, over coconut-shell charcoal.

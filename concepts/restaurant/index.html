Kamayan for groups

Grilled food and rice laid on banana leaves for 4 to 10 people, eaten with your hands. Sets start at ₱5,800. Book two days ahead, and bring the cake if it's a birthday. The upstairs room seats 16 for private dinners.

The kitchen

Chef Migs Santiago grew up beside his lola's ihawan in Bacolod. Baga is the food he missed after nine years in hotel kitchens. The pork comes from Batangas and the fish from Navotas, and both go over coconut-shell charcoal.

The menu is short and changes a little with the market. If something runs out, your server will tell you before you order.

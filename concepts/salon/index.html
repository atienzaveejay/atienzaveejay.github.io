Stylists and levels

A level is set by years behind the chair and advanced training. The cut or color is the same service at every level. You pay more for speed and for harder work like color correction.

Level 1, New talent

Under 2 years, trained here. Lowest prices.

Level 2, Stylist

2 to 5 years. Full color menu.

Level 3, Senior

6 to 12 years. Big changes and blonding.

Level 4, Master

12+ years. Color correction and education.

Prices

Color on hair past the chin adds $20, past the shoulders $40. Your stylist confirms the total before mixing. Going more than two shades lighter, or fixing color from another salon? Start with a free 15-minute consult.

Getting here

TriMet Line 15 stops at SE 30th and Belmont, half a block east. Street parking is free with a two-hour limit on Belmont; side streets have no limit. Bike rack out front.

Access

Step-free entrance and a wide restroom. One wash station has a fully reclining chair. Tell us when you book.

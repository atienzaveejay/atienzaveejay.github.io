Stylists and levels

A level is set by years behind the chair and advanced training. The cut or color is the same service at every level. You pay more for speed and for harder work like color correction.

Level 1, New talent

Under 2 years, trained here. Lowest prices.

Level 2, Stylist

2 to 5 years. Full color menu.

Level 3, Senior

6 to 12 years. Big changes and blonding.

Level 4, Master

12+ years. Color correction and education.

Before you book

Card on file, no charge
We hold a card and charge it after your visit. Appointments over three hours take a $50 deposit, which comes off your total.
Cancelling
Free up to 48 hours before. Inside 48 hours we charge half the service price. Text us at (503) 555-0142 to move a time.
Running late
We can usually work with 15 minutes. Past that we may need to shorten the service or rebook.
First color with us
If you have allergies or reacted to dye before, come in 48 hours early for a free patch test.
Big change? Start with a consult

Going more than two shades lighter, or fixing color from somewhere else: book a free 15-minute consult first. In the studio or on video.

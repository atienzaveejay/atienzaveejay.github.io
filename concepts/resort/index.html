      Learning to surf

      March to June. Small, clean waves on the inside reefs and the warmest water. Lessons are ₱1,500 for 90 minutes with a local instructor and a board.

      Surfing Cloud 9 properly

      August to November, when the bigger swells arrive and the international surf cup runs. Book early: our Surf Lofts fill first.

      Christmas and New Year

      Busy and often rainy, with a minimum stay of four nights from 20 December. Brownouts happen in storms; our generator covers the rooms.

      Questions

      Before you book

      Anything else, message us on Messenger or WhatsApp at +63 900 000 0000.

      How do I pay?

      A 30% deposit holds the room. Pay it by GCash, Maya, bank transfer or card (Visa and Mastercard, 3% fee). The rest is paid at check-out in pesos, cash or card.

      What is the cancellation policy?

      Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. After that the deposit can move to new dates within 12 months. If your flight is cancelled by the airline, we move your booking at no cost.

      What time are check-in and check-out?

      Check-in from 2 PM, check-out by 11 AM. On an early flight you can leave bags and use the outdoor shower before you go.

      Is there an ATM nearby?

      There are two on Tourism Road, five minutes away, but they run out of cash on weekends. Bring enough pesos for tricycles and small cafes.

      Can you book surf lessons and tours?

      Yes, at the front desk or by message before you arrive. Lessons, island hopping, Sugba Lagoon and scooter rental can all go on your room bill.

      Do the rooms have air conditioning?

      All rooms have air conditioning and a ceiling fan. The fans run on the generator during brownouts; the air conditioning comes back once the main power does.
